## 3. Description of the service

A dice roller for tabletop role-playing games: build an expression (dice, modifiers, operators), roll it, and keep a history of the results.

## 4. Access to the service

- No account required.
- A single application, **free with advertising** (Google AdMob, **iOS and Android only** — the macOS version displays none). A **one-off in-app purchase**, "remove ads", disables it: there is no separate paid application to download.

An internet connection is used in two cases only: loading advertising in the free version, and sending a crash report if the Application closes unexpectedly (all versions — see the privacy policy). Neither is needed to roll dice.
